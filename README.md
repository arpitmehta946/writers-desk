# Writer's Desk

A simple phone app for writing articles like a top writer.

**Write an article**
1. Type a topic.
2. Get the expert plan: what the reader really wants, who is searching, and the section-by-section flow with *why* each part goes where it does.
3. Get the full article, with AI-sounding words highlighted and a one-tap fix.
4. Run a quality check (score, what to fix, facts to verify) and fix the issues.

**Change length:** resize any article (for example 4,000 → 1,500 or 800 words) while keeping the answer, key facts and flow. It cuts filler and repetition first, never adds made-up facts, and shows what changed. Works on generated articles and on anything you paste in Improve.

**LinkedIn:** write posts, story posts, carousels, a headline and About section, comments or messages the way a top ghostwriter would. Pick the target group and tone, see a phone preview with the "…see more" cut-off, swap in other openings, tweak it (shorter, punchier hook, more personal) and see why each choice works for that reader. **Expert check** reviews any post (one it wrote or one you paste) like a top ghostwriter: score, hook (recognition, relevance, reward), reader fit, before/after fixes and an authenticity review, with one-tap fixes.

**Improve my writing:** paste anything to see AI-sounding phrases and get an editor's feedback.

Every session starts fresh, and **Start over** clears everything. AI steps run through your Claude app (any plan), or directly if you add a Claude API key in Settings.

Open https://arpitmehta946.github.io/writers-desk/ on your phone and install it (Android: Install app; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).
