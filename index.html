Step 2 · Learn the subject

Research

You don't need to be an expert. You need to know enough to write a complete, accurate article for this reader.

The five-step method

Reader questions

Collect from autocomplete, People Also Ask, reviews, Reddit, forums and support FAQs. Tag each one.

Source log

Primary sources first: provider pages for fees, regulators for rules. If two sources disagree, investigate.

Competitor gaps

Knowledge map

Your outline should grow from these three groups, not from competitor headings.

Step 3 · Audience psychology

Reader profiles

Don't ask what to include. Ask what the reader is trying to achieve, what might stop them, and what will move them forward. Treat assumptions as hypotheses until research backs them.

Same search, different needs

Reader A · In a hurryI need my money today. Where should I go?

Lead with speed, eligibility, opening hours, whether this location can cash it.

Reader B · Cost-consciousHow much will I actually receive?

Lead with fees, comparisons and low-cost alternatives.

Reader C · Large chequeWill they accept it, or will I waste a trip?

Lead with amount limits, verification, cheque types and calling ahead.

Step 4 · The opening

Hook builder

A good hook earns attention. It doesn't hold the answer hostage.

Recognition→Relevance→Reward

Check it against the five techniques

Weak → strong

WeakCheck cashing is a financial service that allows individuals to convert checks into cash.

Correct, but gives no reason to keep reading.

BetterNeed to cash a check today? The best place to go depends on the check you have, the fee you're willing to pay, and whether you have a bank account.

Names the situation and the decision.

StrongestBefore paying a check-cashing fee, compare your bank, the bank that issued the check, and dedicated check-cashing services. The lowest-cost option and the most convenient option may not be the same.

Gives value before asking for more time.
